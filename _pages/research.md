## WiFi-LLM: LLM-Aligned Representation Learning for WiFi CSI Sensing

**Final-Year Project, UESTC** | Sep. 2025 - Jun. 2026  
Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis (Top 10%)

I developed a two-stage framework for signal-to-text human activity recognition: masked self-supervised CSI pretraining followed by feature alignment to a frozen TinyLLaMA-1.1B. The system combines a frequency-aware ResNet encoder, lightweight GRU, and MLP projector. It achieved 92.4% on UT-HAR, 96.2% on NTU-Fi, and 80.3% on WiFall, with analyses of ablation, few-shot transfer, efficiency, and failure cases.

## Magnetic Flux Leakage Sensing for Steel Wire Rope Inspection

**UESTC, supervised by Prof. Zhiliang Liu** | Oct. 2023 - Present

I developed robust defect-detection methods for multi-channel magnetic flux leakage sensing:

- An adaptive multi-scale framework that addresses changes in inspection speed and spatial sampling; it achieved 96.7% F1 on real industrial data and led to a first-author *IEEE Sensors Journal* paper.
- A physics-guided Transformer-CNN reconstruction framework that preserves uniform circular-array topology and reaches 99.4% F1 in fully unsupervised detection at more than 100 FPS.
- Physics-guided methods for angular localization, defect-depth estimation, and radial-basis-function quantification, achieving sub-millimeter localization error and error within one broken wire.

## Human Vital Sign Detection Using 60GHz mmWave Radar

**UESTC, supervised by Prof. Shisheng Guo** | Oct. 2024 - May 2025

I built a non-contact vital-sign sensing pipeline using 60GHz FMCW radar, including range-bin selection, clutter suppression, phase extraction, and motion-artifact mitigation. I applied variational mode decomposition to separate respiration and heartbeat components and evaluated estimates against physiological reference measurements.

## Dual-Task Learning for ECG-Based Identity and Activity Recognition

**UESTC, supervised by Prof. Shisheng Guo** | Oct. 2024 - May 2025

I designed a multi-scale residual convolutional backbone for joint ECG identity and activity representation learning. A subject-adaptive feature modulation mechanism used stable identity features to calibrate activity features, improving cross-subject robustness while retaining real-time inference.
